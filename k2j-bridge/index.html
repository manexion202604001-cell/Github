FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the platform if I don't speak Japanese?

Yes. The platform will support both Korean and Japanese, and you can communicate with your dedicated consultant in Korean. Interpreter support is included for meetings with Japanese partners (wholesalers, retailers, and production companies).

How is trend data collected?

Japanese market reference data uses public rankings and reviews from major Japanese e-commerce malls as the primary source, combined with public social media posts for analysis. Data is collected in compliance with each service's terms of use, and no personal information is gathered. Support for additional e-commerce malls is planned.

How are product concepts and product concept images generated?

The demo dashboard uses generative AI to generate — live, in real time — product concepts, target audiences, pricing plans, regulatory checkpoints, and even packaging color palette suggestions based on e-commerce mall trend data (using your own AI API key, stored only in your browser). The AI is instructed to generate only fully original product and brand names that never duplicate existing brands. Partner designers can then take the generated design direction all the way to print-ready files.

Can I see real reference images in the design proposals?

Yes. The "Design Proposals" tab in the demo dashboard displays real photos in a tiled (masonry) layout (real photography, not illustrations). If you configure your own image search API, industry-wide design trends show real images from design reference sites, and loading a product generated by AI Product Planning or your own product information displays real product photos from major e-commerce malls tailored to that product (royalty-free real photos are used as a fallback when not configured). Note that we do not scrape external sites ourselves — images are retrieved and displayed on the spot through official image search APIs, with links to the source pages provided alongside.

Are the profit simulator, trademark check, and factory quote requests available?

Yes. Proposals generated in the "AI Product Planning" tab include a profit simulator (estimates gross margin and break-even sales volume from target price, cost ratio, monthly sales target, and ad spend), a trademark pre-check link (verify generated brand names via the Japan Patent Office trademark search), and one-click copy of quote request messages for each OEM/ODM factory. Proposals can also be printed or saved as PDF for internal sharing and approvals.

Which regions do the OEM factories cover?

We match you with our network of partner factories in Korea (mainly the Seoul, Gyeonggi, and Chungcheong areas) covering cosmetics, food, and lifestyle goods. Small-lot prototyping (from 1,000 units) is also available.

What about Japanese regulations (the Pharmaceutical and Medical Device Act, the Food Labeling Act)?

We provide regulatory checklists for each category, and on the Develop plan and above you can receive reviews by our partner Japanese specialists (pharmaceutical affairs consultants and licensed administrative scriveners).

Is there a minimum contract period?

The Insight plan can be canceled on a monthly basis. Develop and Launch plans start at 6 months, aligned with the product development cycle.
